# 0024 · 3D print prototype export

Status: accepted · 2026-11-10

## Context
The shells, Core and magnets existed as separate STLs for production intent, but nothing joined the two halves and the parts were not laid out for a desktop printer. Prototype material for now: PLA or ABS on FDM (final material not chosen).

## Decision
`STL · 3D print` builds a separate, print-only casing (`buildCasing({ print: true })`); the preview and every other export are unchanged.
- **Joint:** a rim band (wall + 1.6 mm) around the seam holds a half-lap: the front shell's inner half reaches 2 mm into a rebate in the back shell's inner half, 0.2 mm play. Both shells print dome-up, seam face down, with no overhanging tongue. The lap is on the front so the back shell's seam face is what lies on the plate.
- **Closing:** a snap fit, no closure magnets. The front shell's lap carries a bead sized for a 0.4 mm nozzle: one line wide (0.4 mm proud), 0.8 mm tall, built as a 45° staircase of 0.2 mm layers so it prints without supports and every step is a real layer; it starts 0.4 mm above the lap's tip. It clicks into one plain rectangular groove in the back shell's rebate wall (bead grown by 0.2 mm play; skipped where under 1.4 mm of wall would remain outside it). The rim band is wall + 2.4 mm so the lap is about 1.6 mm wide and prints solid instead of as a hair-thin fin. The rebate in the back shell is only cut where the lap goes (the lap stays inside the cavity at its full depth); where the shell is too shallow for the joint, such as the pointed feet and tips, the band is solid up to the seam, so no sloped wall shows through where a flat rebate floor should be. Magnets are only for the Core: two in the seat floor, and matching pockets in the Core dummy's underside.
- **Core:** seat keeps the 0.3 mm radial clearance; the print includes a Core fit dummy at true puck size so the snug fit is tested before real hardware. The Core's own magnets are 5×2 mm now (were 6×2).
- **Eyes:** engraved 0.6 mm into the front shell (not separate parts).
- **Strap holes:** part of every shape, not an option (the old Loop toggle, ring and protruding flap are gone). Two plain 4 × 3.2 mm vertical slots, 7 mm apart, straight through the back wall near the top; the cord goes in one, runs inside the hollow shell and out the other, and is knotted outside (thread it while the shells are open). Nothing sticks out and nothing is added inside: no channel, no column, so no bridge and no overhang to support (an earlier version had an internal channel under a bar with a solid column around it; its 11 mm roof sagged when printed and closed the passage, so it was dropped). Placement is a rule (`findStrap`): on the centre line, as high as it fits, on a back that faces within 30° of straight back (a vertical cut is then a clean hole, not a long slanted slot), with the body present around the holes, clear of the Core seat, and 8 mm of hollow behind the wall for the cord. If a shape has no room, the sanity check turns red. Tested on all 17 shapes (`tests/strap.test.ts`): a 2.6 mm cord path down one hole, across the hollow and out the other touches neither shell, the holes add no up-facing ceiling near them, both shells lie on a real flat seam ring and are watertight.
- **Layout:** front shell, back shell and Core dummy in a row on z = 0, 8 mm apart, in one STL, **both shells seam face down** (outside surfaces face up and stay clean; supports, if any, sit inside the hollow). Magnets are bought parts and not exported. (A closed-side-down back shell was tried and dropped: the dome rests on a few mm², needs supports under the outside, and the supports roughened the curves.)

## Watertight by construction (all exports)
Bambu Studio first flagged a non-manifold edge. Cause: added material (rim band, bosses, loop lug) was fused to the outer surface, where faces coincide and leave sliver edges. Now extra material is made by filling the empty inside before the hollow is cut (so nothing is fused to the outer face), the lap is kept inside the cavity at its full height, and rim features keep out of the seat cup. The same loop-lug change applies to the normal casing. `src/geometry/meshHealth.ts` (weld by position, every edge in exactly two oppositely wound triangles) is a regression test over every shape, with and without loop, plain and print, and a guard at export time that warns if any STL part would need repair.

## Limits
Tolerances (`src/params/print.ts`) are a first guess for FDM and need one fit-test print; Core and magnet dimensions are still placeholders. The ceiling inside each shell and the free-standing seat walls on the back shell may need light supports. No snap hooks: printed hooks crack in PLA.
